AI in Articulate and Captivate:
5 rules before you click

A practical guide for NHS e-learning developers. Both tools now have AI built in, but that doesn't mean your Trust has cleared it for you to use. Here's what to check, with real NHS examples.

Check before you click

Each Trust decides which AI tools its L&D teams can use. The Trust next door might build its Infection Control refresher with Articulate's AI Assistant, while your IG team still has the same feature switched off.

The good news for your IG team

The first thing IG will ask is "Where does our data go?"

Articulate 360: your content is never used to train AI models. Their AI partners, including OpenAI, delete your text once the output is generated.

Adobe Captivate: your content isn't used to train Adobe's generative AI. IT can switch the AI features off Trust-wide, and users can turn them on or off under Preferences > Generative AI.

The 5 rules

1

Don't go rogue

If the AI features are switched off, don't copy your script into a personal ChatGPT account. Ask IG about adding the tool to your existing DPIA instead.

2

Never use patient data

No real cases, Datix reports or confidential Trust documents. Invent the patient: "Create a scenario about a 72-year-old male presenting with confusion."

3

Respect copyright

Don't paste Royal College textbooks or third-party guidelines into the AI. Write the key points in your own words and link to the source.

4

Check every clinical fact

AI can make up clinical pathways that sound convincing. Check quiz answers against NICE or your Trust's guidance before you publish to ESR.

5

Listen to every voiceover

AI voices still trip over clinical terms, like clopidogrel, or reading "mg" as "m-g". Check AI images reflect the real diversity of NHS staff and patients too.

AI switched off at your Trust?

  1. Email your IG Lead the official AI privacy pages from Articulate or Adobe.
  2. Ask for a DPIA amendment for software your Trust already owns.
  3. Start small, for example drafting non-clinical induction modules first.

Just bear in mind: your Trust's own policy always comes first. If you're not sure what's allowed, ask your Information Governance team before you use any AI feature.
