# AI in Articulate and Captivate: 5 rules before you click

A free guide for NHS e-learning developers who want to use the AI features in Articulate 360 and Adobe Captivate without getting into trouble with Information Governance.

**Live page:** https://garyem.github.io/ai-in-articulate-and-captivate/

## What's in it

Both tools now have AI built in. That doesn't mean your Trust has cleared it for you to use. The guide covers:

- why approval sits with each Trust, not centrally
- what Articulate and Adobe say happens to your data when you use their AI features
- five rules with real NHS examples: don't go rogue, never use patient data, respect copyright, check every clinical fact, and listen to every voiceover
- what to do if AI is switched off at your Trust, including how to ask your IG Lead for a DPIA amendment

## Using it

It's free to use and share with your team. No sign-up and no email needed.

One thing to bear in mind: your Trust's own policy always comes first. If you're not sure what's allowed, check with your Information Governance team before you use any AI feature.
